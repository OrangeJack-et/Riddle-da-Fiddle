      📖 How to play Riddle-da-Fiddle

      Quick summary

      • Everyone guesses how many cards of a type are in all hands put together.
      • On your turn: guess higher, fiddle (the last guess is too high) or riddle (the last guess is exactly right).
      • Fiddle: whoever was wrong loses 1 card. Riddle: right = +1 card, wrong = −2 cards.
      • Your cards are your lives. Run out and you're out. Last player with cards wins.

      The cards

      A normal 52-card deck plus 2 jokers. Every card is one of three types:

      Lower2 – 6
      Middle7 – 10
      FaceJ, Q, K
      • Aces are wild: they count as Lower, Middle and Face.
      • Jokers are flexible: they can count towards whatever was guessed, or not (see Jokers below).

      Each round

      • Everyone starts with 6 cards. Your number of cards is also your number of lives.
      • Every round the whole deck is shuffled and you're dealt as many cards as you have lives. You only see your own hand.
      • The most cards you can have is 8.
      • With lots of players near 8 cards the deck can run out; then the biggest hands are left a card or two short.

      Your turn

      The first player of a round must make a guess. After that, on your turn you do one of these:

      Guess
      Claim there are at least that many cards of a type across everyone's hands, e.g. "Middle 4". Your guess must be higher than the last one: a bigger number, or the same number with a higher type (Lower < Middle < Face).
      Middle 4 beats Lower 4 · Lower 5 beats Face 4 · Lower 4 does not beat Middle 4
      You can't guess more cards than there are in play (shown on the table).
      Fiddle
      You think the last guess is wrong (there aren't that many). Everyone's cards are revealed and counted:
      • at least that many: the guess was true, so you lose 1 card;
      • fewer: the guess was false, so the guesser loses 1 card.
      Sam guessed Face 3. You fiddle and there are 4 Face cards: the guess was true, you lose a card.
      Riddle
      You think the last guess is exactly right. Everyone's cards are revealed and counted:
      • exactly that many: you gain 1 card (if you're already on 8, nothing happens);
      • any other number: you lose 2 cards.
      Sam guessed Lower 5. You riddle and there are exactly 5 Lower cards: you go up a card.

      A fiddle or riddle ends the round. Then the cards are reshuffled and dealt again.

      Jokers

      • On a fiddle: everyone holding jokers secretly chooses how many of theirs count towards the guess (from none to all of them). Everyone chooses at the same time.
      • On a riddle: jokers are wild. They can count as any number from none to all of them, so the riddle is right if the guess is anywhere in that range.
      • Guess Middle 6, there are 5 Middle cards and 1 joker in play. Riddle: right (the joker makes it 6). Fiddle: depends whether the joker holder counts it.

      Who starts the next round

      The first round starts with the first player. After that, whoever lost the last round starts:

      • Fiddle: whoever lost a card.
      • Riddle right: the player whose guess got riddled.
      • Riddle wrong: the riddler.

      If that player has just been knocked out, the next player still in starts.

      Last Stand

      Whenever a player drops to 1 card, the next round is their Last Stand. They start it, and the card type of their first guess is locked for the whole round: if they say Face, everyone has to keep guessing Face (just higher numbers). Fiddles and riddles work as normal.

      • It happens every time someone drops to 1 card, however they got there (a lost fiddle or a wrong riddle).
      • If they survive, the round after that is back to normal.

      Winning

      Lose all your cards and you're out (you can keep watching and chatting). The last player with cards wins.

      On this website

      • Results screen: after every fiddle or riddle you see everyone's hand, with the cards that counted highlighted. It stays up for 10 seconds, or until everyone clicks Ready.
      • Bots: the host can add Noob (plays randomly), Pro (plays the odds) and Hacker (cheats: sees everyone's cards!) bots in the lobby.
      • Disconnects: if you lose connection, you have 30 seconds to come back (just reopen the page). After that a Pro bot plays for you until you return.
      • Leaving: if you click Leave mid-game, a bot takes your seat for the rest of that game.
      • Host: whoever made the game. They can add and remove bots and players, start the game, put a disconnected player's seat on a bot straight away, and end the game.
      • Only bots left: the game ends and the bot with the most cards wins.
      • Chat: type in the box under the log. Messages are up to 200 characters.
      • Music: 🔊 in the top bar turns the background music on or off (just for you).
